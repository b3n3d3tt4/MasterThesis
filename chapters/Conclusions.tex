\chapter{Conclusions}


Further improvements will be for sure carried out in the future: regarding the \ac{VAE} algorithm the main goal will be the modification of the classifier layer, tuning labels with a probability of being such. Moreover, another way of using this model is as a denoising algorithm to clean waveforms: in this case a comparison between parameters computed from the original and the reconstructed waveforms can be performed. Concerning the \ac{DTC} model, implementing the heatmap architecture outlined by the authors, would make it possible to highlight features that are most relevant in the clustering process of the algorithm.

Moreover, other deep learning strategies could be investigated, for example a \ac{SOM} model.
