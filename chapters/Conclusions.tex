\chapter{Conclusions}


Further improvements will be for sure carried out in the future. Regarding the \ac{VAE} algorithm the main goal will be the modification of the classifier layer, tuning labels with a probability of being such. Concerning the \ac{DTC} model, implementing the heatmap architecture outlined by the authors, would make it possible to highlight features that are most relevant in the clustering process of the algorithm. Moreover the \ac{TAE} has a deterministic latent space with no regularisation: any information that lowers the reconstruction loss can be stored in it, including the event-by-event fluctuations of the baseline. The reconstructions obtained after the pre-training suggest that part of this noise is indeed encoded, since small oscillations of the baseline are reproduced, whereas they are not reproduced by the \ac{VAE}, whose latent space is regularised by the \ac{KL} term. Encoded noise enters the distances between the latent sequences and the centroids, and can therefore degrade the clustering. A natural development is a variational version of the temporal autoencoder, in which the encoder outputs a mean and a variance for every time step of the latent sequence and a \ac{KL} term penalises the information stored in it, so that only the features that are relevant for the reconstruction of the pulse shape are kept. The clustering layer would then operate on the means of the latent sequences, combining the temporal structure of the \ac{DTC} latent space with the regularisation of the \ac{VAE}.

Furthermore, other machine learning strategies could be investigated, for example a \ac{SOM} model.
