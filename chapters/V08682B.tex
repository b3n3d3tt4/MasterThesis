\subsection{Data selection}

A similar contamination between the \ac{SSE} and \ac{MSE} proxy samples is observed for detector V08682B, driven by the non-negligible continuous background underneath both the $^{208}\text{Tl}$ \ac{DEP} and $^{212}\text{Bi}$ \ac{FEP} peaks (Figure~\ref{fig:fits-and-cuts-V08682B}). Despite this common systematic feature, the overall sample purity is notably improved compared to detector V08682A, as summarized in Table~\ref{tab:sse_mse_counts-V08682B}. This enhanced performance is largely attributable to the prominent intensity of the \ac{DEP} peak relative to the continuum. From a physical standpoint, the smaller active volume of V08682B naturally increases the probability that both $511\text{ keV}$ annihilation photons escape without undergoing secondary Compton interactions within the bulk. This higher double-escape efficiency boosts the relative yield of true single-site depositions, yielding a cleaner \ac{SSE} training set across all selected energy intervals.

\subsection{Variational Autoencoder}

As shown by the reconstructed waveforms and the training dynamics, the \ac{VAE} architecture demonstrates robust reconstruction performance on both the validation and the test sets. The network accurately reproduces the overall pulse shapes, capturing the fast rising edges and peak amplitudes of the current signals without introducing discernible artifacts or smoothing high-frequency features excessively. The generalization capability is corroborated by the loss metrics evaluated across the datasets. As reported in Table~\ref{tab:vae_3sigma_B} and Table~\ref{tab:vae_1sigma_B}, the total loss, the reconstruction loss (\ac{NLL}), and the \ac{KL} divergence converge steadily and remain consistent among the training, validation, and test subsets for both the $\pm 3\sigma$ and $\pm 1\sigma$ selection windows, indicating no noticeable overfitting. Overall, these reconstruction results are in close agreement with those previously observed for detector V08682A, confirming the stability of the \ac{VAE} for different detector geometries.

For both selection windows, the classification accuracy reached on V08682B is higher than on V08682A. The two detectors share crystal, enrichment, electronics and operating voltage, so the difference originates from their size. Size, however, acts on several factors at once: the purity of the proxy samples (Table~\ref{tab:sse_mse_counts-V08682B}), the pulse shapes themselves through shorter drift paths, and the number of training events. Moreover, accuracy is measured against contaminated labels and therefore largely reflects their purity, as also shown within each detector by the higher accuracy of the $\pm1\sigma$ windows with respect to the $\pm3\sigma$ ones. As discussed in Chapter 4, a higher accuracy does not imply a better SSE/MSE discrimination, which is in fact weaker for V08682B than for V08682A (Table 4.1).

\subsubsection{$\gamma$ scan}
Following the same modalities of Sec.~\ref{subsec:train-test-DTC} a scan over the value of $\gamma$ and over the number of clusters ($K$) has been performed investigating $\gamma=10, 100, 1000, 10\,000$ and $K=2, 3, 4$. In particular what we can observ is that with $\gamma=10, 100$ the clustering term of the loss is far too weak to get a proper clustering of events. For $\gamma=1000, 10\,000$, on the contrary, a good clustering is achieved (see Fig. \ref{fig:scan-gamma-V08682B}): nonetheless also in this case the choice fell upon $\gamma=1000$ which is evidently the most stable scenario since for $10\,000$ the scan highlights an unstable clustering loss and a high number of events changing clusters between one epoch and another as is visible from Appendix \ref{app:DTC-scans-B} in Fig.~\ref{fig:scan-reconstruction_and_losses-gamma10000-B}.

\subsubsection{$K$ scan}

The $K$-scan confirmed the observations made for V08682A, indicating that the optimal number of clusters is $2$. Specifically, for $K=3$, no events were assigned to the third cluster—consistent with the V08682A case. Furthermore, while the $K=4$ scan for V08682A populated a third cluster with a few events, in this case only two clusters were filled, leaving the remaining two completely empty at the end of training.
